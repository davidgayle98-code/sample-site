Welcome In

Your New Neighborhood Spot

Angelina's is a locally owned family restaurant right on York Street. We serve food off the grill, Italian favorites from the kitchen, craft cocktails, wine, and cold beer, in a roomy dining room with a lively bar.

Come in for lunch, grab a seat at the bar for the game, or bring the whole family for dinner. There's something on the menu for everybody, including the kids.
