Our story

A Real Art Store, Run by Artists' Neighbors

GBS Art Depot opened its doors in Mechanicsburg in the summer of 1993. More than thirty years later, we're still family owned, and our goal hasn't changed: give local artists the best materials available at prices that don't break the bank.

You won't find us in a strip of big-box stores. Look for the hand-painted sign and the graffiti mural on North Arch Street. Inside, our staff can answer questions, recommend products, and help you find exactly what your project needs.

Gift certificates available, the perfect present for the artist in your life.
