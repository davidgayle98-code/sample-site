You'll know us by the striped awnings on the corner of East Main Street. We've been part of downtown Mechanicsburg for more than thirty years, and we still do things the hometown way: no pressure, no rush, and honest answers to every question.

Take your time at the cases. Try things on. Bring in the ring that needs sizing or the piece you've been meaning to fix. Whether you're here for an engagement ring or a birthday gift, you'll get the same personal attention.
