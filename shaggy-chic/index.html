What we do

Grooming Services

Every pet gets a bath and blow dry. From there, we tailor the groom to your pet's breed, coat, and how they're feeling that day.

Bath & Blow Dry

A full bath, rinse, and fluff dry. Ask about our natural shampoo option for sensitive skin.

Full Groom & Haircut

Bath plus a breed-style or custom haircut, trimmed the way you like it.

Cat Grooming

Baths, brush-outs, and trims for cats, handled calmly and at their pace.

Puppy's First Groom

A short, gentle intro to the tub, the dryer, and the table so grooming stays stress free for life.

De-Shedding

A deep brush-out to pull loose undercoat and cut down on shedding at home.

Nails, Ears & Teeth

Nail trims, ear cleaning, and teeth brushing, on their own or added to any groom.

What to Expect

  1. Book ahead. Call us or send a request below with your pet's breed, size, and what you'd like done.
  2. Get a price up front. Grooming prices depend on size, coat, and condition. We'll quote you before we start, and tell you what any add-ons cost.
  3. Drop off. Tell us about any sensitive spots, health issues, or nerves so we can go at your pet's pace.
  4. Pick up. We'll call when your pet is ready, and share any tips on matting, skin, or nails we noticed.
